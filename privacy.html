Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

This policy explains what information the Cini app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email cinisupport@gmail.com.

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, username, and password (stored securely by our authentication provider; we never see it in plain text). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address and, if you allow it, your name.
  • Profile: an optional display name, bio and profile photo.
  • Phone number (optional): only if you use "Find friends from contacts". We verify it with a text message and keep it with your login details, where other users cannot see it. You can remove it at any time in Settings.
  • What you post in the app: your rankings and scores, notes and thoughts, watchlist, watch dates, what you are currently watching, watch plans, comments, likes, reactions, the people you tag as "watched with", and stills or GIFs you attach to posts.
  • Social connections: who you follow, follow requests, and accounts you block.
  • Reports: if you report a post, comment or account, we keep the report and any note you add so we can review it.

Information collected automatically

  • Push notification token: if you turn on notifications, we store a device token so we can send you notifications. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Finding friends from your contacts

This is optional, and only happens if you choose it and allow access to your contacts.

  • Cini reads only the phone numbers in your contacts, not names, emails or anything else.
  • On your phone, each number is put in a standard format and turned into a code (a one-way hash) before anything is sent.
  • We compare those codes with the verified numbers of people on Cini and show you the matches. We do not store your contacts or the codes we receive, whether they matched anyone or not.
  • Hashing helps, but phone numbers can in principle be worked out from their codes, so we also limit how often contacts can be checked and keep nothing afterwards.
  • You only appear in someone else's matches if you have verified your number and left "Let people find me by phone number" turned on in Settings. Blocked accounts never see you.

How we use your information

  • To run your account and show your rankings, lists and activity.
  • To show your posts to the people allowed to see them, and to send you notifications about activity involving you.
  • To recommend titles based on what you have ranked.
  • To keep Cini safe: reviewing reports, removing content that breaks our Terms of Use, and removing abusive accounts.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

Who can see what you post

  • Your username, display name and profile photo are visible to other Cini users.
  • Your rankings are visible to other users unless you make your account private, in which case only people you approve can see them.
  • Your activity posts are visible to your followers. Your watchlist is visible only to people you follow who also follow you back.
  • If you block someone, you and that person can no longer see each other's profiles or posts.

Services we rely on

We use a small number of service providers to run Cini. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, sign-in and file storage (including profile photos).
  • Apple and Google: if you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, they confirm your identity to us.
  • Expo: delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Our SMS provider: sends the verification text when you add a phone number.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database): provides movie and TV information and images. No personal information is sent to TMDB.
  • KLIPY: provides GIF search. When you search for a GIF, your search terms and an anonymous account identifier are sent to KLIPY.
  • YouTube: trailers play in an embedded YouTube player, which is subject to Google's privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile and everything you have posted, including rankings, lists, comments and follows. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke Cini's access to your Apple ID.

You can also edit or delete individual posts, notes and comments at any time, or email us to ask for a copy of your data or for help deleting it.

Children

Cini is not intended for children under 13, and you must be 13 or older to create an account. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access controls so that people can only see what they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and let you know in the app.
